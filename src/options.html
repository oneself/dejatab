DejaTab

One tab per page, and you decide what counts as a page. Changes take effect at once, no restart.

Where DejaTab acts

No sites are allowed, so DejaTab does nothing. Add a site under Allowed sites.

Matching rules

Two tabs are the same page when every rule you switch on, applied to both addresses, leaves them equal.

Tracking parameters

Allowed sites

In 'Only the allowed sites' mode, DejaTab acts on these sites and nothing else. An entry covers the site and everything under it. Excluded sites stay excluded even when listed here.

These have no effect while DejaTab acts on every site.

Excluded sites

DejaTab ignores these entirely: it never closes their tabs and never asks about them. An entry covers the site and everything under it. Answering Never in the prompt adds a site here.

Per-site rules

A site starts from the rules above and changes only what it names. The most specific entry wins, so mail.example.com beats example.com.

Answering Always in the prompt adds one for you.
